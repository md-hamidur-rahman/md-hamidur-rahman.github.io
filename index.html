Biography

Md. Hamidur Rahman is an Assistant Library Officer at North South University, Bangladesh. He holds a Master of Social Science (MSS) in Information Science and Library Management from Noakhali Science and Technology University, where he graduated with a CGPA of 3.93 out of 4.00. He also earned his bachelor's degree in the same field from NSTU, with a CGPA of 3.57.

His professional experience includes library services and information management, with previous internships at the Bangladesh National Scientific and Technical Documentation Center (BANSDOC) and Dhaka University Library. His research interests include digital libraries, information access, information literacy, human-computer interaction (HCI), user experience design, and privacy and security in information systems.

Beyond his library and academic work, Hamidur is interested in technology and digital innovation. He has developed skills in web design, data analysis, and digital tools, and is familiar with platforms and software such as Koha, DSpace, SPSS, Python, and Figma.

Recognized for his academic performance through merit-based scholarships, he continues to explore research opportunities and prepare for doctoral study, with the goal of contributing to research and innovation in information science.
